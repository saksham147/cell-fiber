Printing Paper

Woodfree Uncoated Paper
Image

Woodfree uncoated paper, also known as offset paper, is available in a wide range of grammages and colours.

Lower grammages are ideal for fine book printing, such as Bibles and Qurans, while 75 and 80 GSM papers are ideal for office printing and writing applications, including A4 copier paper.

Higher grammages are commonly used for boards, packaging materials, and promotional products such as business cards.

The most common applications include office printing and writing papers, books, stationery, and promotional items.

Woodfree Coated Paper
Image

Woodfree coated paper is available in grammages ranging from 80 GSM to 400 GSM. This grade is ideal for high-end magazines, promotional materials, and stationery products such as calendars.

It is available in gloss, silk, and matte finishes, with brightness levels starting at 90% or higher. The coating can be applied to one side (C1S) or both sides (C2S).

Book Paper
Image

Bulky book paper is an uncoated graphical paper grade suitable for printing books and publications, as well as manufacturing stationery products.

We offer bulky book paper in a range of bulk and caliper options, with grammages ranging from 50 GSM to 90 GSM. It is available in white, creamy, and bluish shades.

Copier Paper
Image

We offer A4 copier paper in grammages ranging from 70 GSM to 90 GSM, with various brightness levels starting at 90%.

Its most common applications include stationery and everyday office printing.
