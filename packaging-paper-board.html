Kraft Grades

We take pride in delivering high-quality paper and packaging products to customers worldwide. Our range of kraft paper solutions offers unique properties to meet the diverse requirements of various industries.

Kraftliner Board (KLB)
Image

A high-grammage paper used in the production of boxes and corrugated packaging. KLB is a strong and durable material designed to withstand challenging conditions, including exposure to moisture and heavy pressure.

Coated & Uncoated White Top Kraft Liner
Image

White top liners are designed to meet the requirements of premium packaging, offering a bright, high-quality surface and excellent printability.

Semi Chemical Fluting
Image

Semi-chemical flutings are developed to deliver reliable performance under demanding conditions. Primarily made from fresh fibres, they offer excellent strength, dimensional stability, and humidity resistance, helping protect products during transportation, cold storage, and humid environments.

Natural Kraft
Image

Unbleached or natural kraft paper is best known for its high strength and is predominantly brown in colour. It is a popular choice for various applications, particularly in the food industry for food wrapping and packaging, as well as for manufacturing kraft paper bags.

Bleached Kraft
Image

Many industries choose bleached kraft paper for its high strength and smooth, attractive finish. This type of kraft paper is commonly used to manufacture shopping bags, sugar bags, flour bags, and milk bags. It meets the needs of end users who value both durability and visual appeal.

MG Kraft
Image

MG kraft offers qualities similar to machine-finished (MF) kraft but features a smooth, glossy finish on one side due to its special coating. This coating enhances rigidity and durability while improving printability, making MG kraft an ideal choice for shopping bags, boutique bags, and flexible packaging applications.
